# Design Context — Brio OS ⚡

## 1. Essence & Creator Archetype
- **Archetype**: **The Modern Scholar-Engineer** (*El Investigador-Artesano de Sistemas*).
- **Core Philosophy**:
  - Obsession with flow optimization, keyboard-first speed, and frictionless execution.
  - Cognitive silence: zero decorative visual noise, maximum mental clarity.
  - Deep work & intellectual depth: space for long-form notes, ambitious metrics, and deliberate reflection.
  - Physical reference: Modern research library, Midori/Leuchtturm archival paper, fountain pen precision, specialty coffee, and tactile mechanical instruments.

---

## 2. Aesthetic System: Warm Archival Noir
A bespoke, tactile, editorial-technical design language that completely rejects generic 2024-2025 AI aesthetic tropes (no purple/indigo blurred glow orbs, no cold `#090a0f` void, no repetitive frosted glass pills).

### Palette & Color Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (Warm Charcoal / Ebony)**: `#121110` (Dark, organic, warm-tinted neutral; never pure #000 or cold #090a0f).
- **Surface Elevation (Archival Layers)**:
  - Surface 1 (Base Panels / Ledger): `#181715`
  - Surface 2 (Hover / Active Fields): `#22201D`
  - Surface 3 (Floating Overlays / Modals): `#282622`
- **Typography & Inks (Parchment & Ivory)**:
  - High-Contrast Text (Headings / Key Metrics): `#F5F2EB` (Warm cream/ivory)
  - Primary Body Text: `#DDD6C9` (Soft parchment)
  - Secondary / Muted Text: `#8E867B` (Warm stone/graphite)
  - Hairline Borders & Rulers: `#2A2723` (Crisp, warm 1px dividers)
- **Semantic Inks (Refined Natural Accents)**:
  - **Focus & Gold / EXP**: Warm Amber / Ochre (`#D99B43` / `#E8AF59`)
  - **Health & Critical Action**: Lapis Coral / Lacquer Red (`#E05D52`)
  - **Mana & Energy**: Sage Cyan / Muted Teal (`#4EAB9E` or `#59B4C3`)
  - **Success / Consistent Streak**: Forest Olive / Spring Moss (`#7EA35A`)
  - **Habitica / RPG Badges**: Deep Warm Terracotta & Burnished Brass

---

## 3. Typography Hierarchy
1. **Display & Editorial Headings (`font-serif`)**:
   - Primary: **Fraunces** or **Newsreader** (Google Fonts).
   - Usage: Ritual headers (Morning/Evening), big numbers/metrics, view titles, modal hero statements.
2. **Interface & Reading (`font-sans`)**:
   - Primary: **Plus Jakarta Sans** / **Satoshi**.
   - Usage: Body copy, task descriptions, menus, input labels, tooltips.
3. **Telemetry, Ledger & Code (`font-mono`)**:
   - Primary: **JetBrains Mono** / **IBM Plex Mono**.
   - Usage: Keyboard shortcuts (<kbd>⌘1</kbd>, <kbd>j/k</kbd>), financial amounts ($MXN), HP/MP percentages, timestamps, timer countdowns.

---

## 4. Visual Structure & Layout (No More Card Slop)
- **Archival Ledger & Rule Lines**: Information is organized using crisp 1px hairline rules (`border-[#2A2723]`) and typographic rhythm rather than nesting cards inside cards.
- **Debossed / Letterpress Badges**: Small metadata tags styled like archival catalog stamps or bookplate labels (`border border-[#38332D] bg-[#1C1A17] text-[#C2BAAD]`).
- **Tactile Progress Gauges**: High-precision segmented or solid ink lines instead of generic rainbow gradients.
- **Marginalia & Details**: Subtle metadata placed with intention, utilizing tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for perfect alignment.

---

## 5. Interaction & Motion Physics
- **Mechanical Precision**: Transitions use fast, calibrated deceleration (e.g. `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` ~150ms-220ms).
- **Zero Cartoon Bounce**: No playful rubber-band springs. Everything feels like a finely tuned Leica lens, high-end mechanical watch, or custom mechanical keyboard switch.
- **Audio Sensory Grounding**: Deep, organic acoustic feedback (warm wood clicks, soft mechanical relays, meditative analog white/brown noise).
